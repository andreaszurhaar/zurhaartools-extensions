10 Best Hiking Trails in the Alps

By Marco Rossi · April 20, 2026 · 12 min read

The Alps offer some of the most breathtaking hiking experiences in Europe. Whether you're a seasoned mountaineer or a casual day-hiker, there's a trail for everyone. Here are our top picks for the 2026 season.

1. Tour du Mont Blanc

This iconic 170km trail circles the Mont Blanc massif through France, Italy, and Switzerland. Most hikers complete it in 10-12 days, staying in mountain refuges along the way. The views of glaciers and alpine meadows are unparalleled.

2. Haute Route (Chamonix to Zermatt)

A classic high-altitude traverse covering 180km through the Swiss and French Alps. This challenging route rewards hikers with views of the Matterhorn, Monte Rosa, and dozens of other peaks above 4,000 meters.

3. Eagle Walk (Adlerweg), Austria

Stretching 413km across Tirol, the Eagle Walk is divided into 33 stages. Each stage offers different terrain, from lush valleys to rocky ridgelines. It's a fantastic way to experience the diversity of the Austrian Alps.

4. Dolomites Alta Via 1

A 120km trek through the stunning rock formations of the Italian Dolomites. The route passes through dramatic landscapes with towering pinnacles, green valleys, and wartime history. Allow 8-10 days.

5. Bernese Oberland Trail

This Swiss classic takes you past the famous trio of Eiger, Monch, and Jungfrau. The well-maintained paths and excellent mountain huts make it accessible to hikers of moderate fitness.

Whether you choose a multi-day expedition or a gentle day hike, the Alps never disappoint. Remember to check weather conditions, carry appropriate gear, and respect local wildlife and vegetation.

Essential Gear Checklist

  • Waterproof hiking boots with ankle support
  • Layered clothing system (base, insulation, shell)
  • Trekking poles for steep descents
  • Map, compass, and GPS device
  • First aid kit and emergency whistle
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses (UV is stronger at altitude)
  • Refillable water bottle and purification tablets
