Backend Engineer

Engineering · Amsterdam, Netherlands · Full-time

Job Description

CloudNine is building the next generation of cloud infrastructure tools. We're looking for a Backend Engineer who is passionate about building scalable, reliable systems that power thousands of businesses worldwide.

As part of our engineering team, you will design and implement core platform services, work on our API layer, and help shape the technical direction of our product. This is an opportunity to work on challenging distributed systems problems at scale.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design and implement microservices using Go and Python
  • Build and maintain RESTful and GraphQL APIs
  • Work with PostgreSQL, Redis, and Elasticsearch for data storage and retrieval
  • Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions
  • Participate in on-call rotation and incident response
  • Write comprehensive tests and documentation
  • Contribute to system design and architecture reviews

Requirements

  • 4+ years of backend development experience
  • Strong proficiency in Go, Python, or Java
  • Experience with relational databases and query optimization
  • Understanding of distributed systems concepts (consensus, replication, partitioning)
  • Experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes)
  • Familiarity with cloud providers (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills

Nice to have

  • Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, RabbitMQ)
  • Knowledge of infrastructure as code (Terraform, Pulumi)
  • Contributions to open source projects
  • Experience working in a startup or high-growth environment

What We Offer

  • Salary: €70,000 - €95,000 depending on experience
  • Employee stock options
  • Hybrid working (2 days in office per week)
  • 28 vacation days
  • Learning budget of €2,500 per year
  • Top-tier equipment and ergonomic home office setup
  • Pension scheme and health insurance
  • Weekly team lunches and monthly social events

CloudNine is committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace. We welcome applications from candidates of all backgrounds.
