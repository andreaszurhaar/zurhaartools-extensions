About the job

We are looking for an experienced Data Scientist to join our growing team at TechCorp. In this role, you will be responsible for developing and implementing machine learning models, analyzing large datasets, and providing actionable insights to drive business decisions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and deploy machine learning models to solve complex business problems
  • Analyze large datasets using Python, SQL, and statistical methods to uncover trends and patterns
  • Collaborate with product managers and engineers to integrate data-driven solutions into our platform
  • Build and maintain data pipelines for model training and evaluation
  • Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders at all levels of the organization
  • Stay current with the latest research in machine learning and data science
  • Mentor junior team members and contribute to the team's technical growth

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in data science or machine learning
  • Strong proficiency in Python, R, or similar programming languages
  • Experience with machine learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn)
  • Solid understanding of statistical analysis and experimental design
  • Experience with SQL and large-scale data processing tools (Spark, Hadoop)
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain complex concepts to non-technical audiences
  • MS or PhD in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field preferred

Nice to Have

  • Experience with deep learning and natural language processing
  • Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
  • Experience with A/B testing and causal inference
  • Publications in relevant academic conferences or journals

What We Offer

  • Competitive salary range: €80,000 - €120,000 depending on experience
  • Hybrid working model (3 days office, 2 days remote)
  • 30 days paid vacation
  • Professional development budget of €3,000 per year
  • Stock options and performance bonuses
  • Health insurance and pension contributions
  • Modern office in Amsterdam with standing desks and free lunch

TechCorp is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
